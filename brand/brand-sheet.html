Midnight, again and again. A clock that rolls over midnight on a loop. Black screen, thin white digits, one moment in ember.

The mark

A ring with a gap at 12 o'clock and one dot in the gap. The ring is the loop. The dot is midnight.

Lockup

The wordmark is rollover, lowercase, in Inter Light with −2 % tracking. Next to the type the ring gets heavier (0.19 r) so it matches the letter stems.
